# OurPatch — first email reminder post

Draft social post celebrating the first automated weekly email from OurPatch
(13 August 2026). Primary version written for LinkedIn; short version for X.

---

## LinkedIn

An app I built emailed me last night — and I'd completely forgotten it was going to.

Coming into spring I was frustrated. I want our garden to grow more of our food, but I never really know what needs doing, or when. Gardening advice is always written for someone else's climate and someone else's backyard.

So I built OurPatch. It looks at:

→ your geographic location
→ your climate
→ every one of your garden beds and what's growing in them
→ your growing preferences

and breaks it all down into a really simple, prioritised plan: this is what to do, and when.

I onboarded our beds last Thursday, then promptly forgot about it. Yesterday I caught myself thinking, geez — what do I actually need to do this weekend?

At 7:01pm the answer landed in our inbox:

"Late winter in Bellingen still has a bite in the morning air, but the wattle's out and the soil knows spring's coming."

If you only do one thing this week (~45 min): weed and mulch the Round stone bed, and harvest the ready ginger and peppers. Plus a nudge that it's the last direct sowing of the season for peas, and easy wins right now with radish and rocket.

No dashboard to check. No app to remember to open. Just the right advice, for our exact patch, exactly when I needed it.

That's the moment I build for — when something you made quietly starts working for you.

(Built solo, AI-assisted, like everything else I ship.)
